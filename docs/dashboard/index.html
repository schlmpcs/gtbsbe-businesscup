What we're building

A forward-looking budget planner for US adults aged 25–45, with a daily guessing game as the marketing engine.

Checked against origin/main @ fd3be2d (15 Nov 2026, 11:23 GMT+5) and 209 GitHub issues and pull requests.

The pitch

Money apps forecast your accounts. We forecast your decisions, and rank which one to make next.

Nobody can feel what their life costs, or where small choices lead. The game proves it in a minute. The app fixes it.
