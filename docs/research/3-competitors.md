## How this was researched (read first)

- Only web *search* worked. Direct page fetches failed because monarch.com, sacra.com and costbench.com are outside this session's network allowlist, so primary pages could not be opened. Each fact below comes from search-result snippets of the URL cited. Before anything goes on a pitch slide, open the source in a browser.
- Labels used: **VERIFIED-ish** means a primary or company source appeared in the results (press release, official help page, company blog). **SECONDARY** means aggregator, review or affiliate sites, often disagreeing with each other. **UNVERIFIED** means one weak source, conflicting sources, or nothing found.
- Pricing aggregators (costbench, fincomparelab, getfinny, toolradar, Gerald) often disagree. Wherever they conflict, the range is given rather than one number.

### ChatGPT "Finances" (new, important)
- OpenAI launched personal finance in ChatGPT on **May 15, 2026**, as a US preview for **Pro** subscribers. It links banks through Plaid (12,000+ institutions) and can see balances, spending, subscriptions, upcoming payments, portfolios and liabilities. Access is read-only. ## 2. Finelo (Zimran): what it does, so we don't copy it

- **What it is:** "Finelo: Master Trading", an **investing and trading education app**. It offers bite-sized daily lessons, **28-day challenges**, a **market simulator with virtual funds** (reportedly $1,000 starting balance, real market data), "AI tools for market analysis", and **streaks and achievements**. The company says it is "an educational product, not a brokerage".
  - https://finelo.com/app , https://apps.apple.com/us/app/finelo-master-trading/id6670422243
- **Price:** Web-funnel subscription (4-week plan from ~$19.99. Reviews also cite $6.99/1 week and $39.99/12 weeks). In-app purchases range $9.99–$119.99. There is no free trial (SECONDARY and conflicting): https://finelo.com/pricing , https://plisio.net/profiles/finelo-review
- **Scale:** Company press release (Feb 2026) claims 1.5M+ learners and 8.8M lessons completed (company claim): https://fortune.com/press-releases/finelo-1-5-million-learners-ai-financial-education-us-debt-19-trillion-2026-02-25
- **Zimran's portfolio:**
  - **Coursiv**, an "AI Gym" for AI skills. A secondary estimate says ~$1.8M in its first year, which is **UNVERIFIED**.
  - **Prosperi Academy**, also investing education plus a simulator.
  - **SubPilot: Cancel Subscriptions**, published under the Finelo Limited developer account. It overlaps with Rocket Money's core feature.
  - Sources: https://trademarks.justia.com/owners/zimran-limited-5716764 , https://www.appbrain.com/dev/Finelo+Limited/
  - No Zimran budgeting or forecasting app was found.
- **Avoid copying these Zimran patterns:**
  1. Investing or trading education, stock simulators, or "learn to invest" courses.
  2. A lessons-plus-streaks "Duolingo for money" loop. Our daily game should be a guessing game about cost of living, not a lesson streak, and we should not lead with "streaks and achievements" as the hook.
  3. Subscription-cancellation features (SubPilot).
  4. Web-to-app quiz funnels with aggressive upsell ladders. Zimran is known for these, and press and reviewers have criticized billing practices (see the UK ASA ruling on Prosperi Academy, per an unverified doc). Keep our paywall honest and transparent.
- **Where we're clearly different from Finelo:** Our product is household cash flow and decisions (cars, loans, rent, kids), not markets. We do not touch securities. Our game is about *cost of living*, not stock picking.

**Verdict on the claim "no competitor has a game built on users' own finances":**
- **Among the money apps in section 1, the claim holds.** None of Monarch, YNAB, Copilot, Rocket Money, Cleo, PocketSmith, ProjectionLab, Boldin, Empower, Origin, Simplifi, Credit Karma, NerdWallet or Finelo has a guessing game whose content is other users' (anonymized) budgets.
- **Across the wider market, it needs care.** User-submitted finances as *content* already exist (Money Diaries, Salary Transparent Street, twocents), and price- and rent-guessing games are a crowded "-dle" genre. We found **no** game where players guess a stranger's monthly cost of living from submitted, anonymized profiles. Absence of evidence is not proof, so say "we found none", not "none exist".
- **MoneyRank is the closest conceptual match to our "rank moves".** It is a tiny Product Hunt project (4–8 followers), with generic scenarios and no personal forecast. Be ready to answer "isn't that MoneyRank?" with "theirs is opinion polling on generic choices; ours ranks *your* moves by computed 10-year $".

## 4. Decision-focused tools vs "rank moves in dollars"

| Tool type | Example | What it answers | Gap vs us |
|---|---|---|---|
| Single-purpose calculators | NerdWallet auto loan / "how much car" / personal loan; Bankrate auto loan & affordability | One decision at a time, payment or rate math | No profile carried across decisions, no 10-year net-worth effect, no comparison *between* different kinds of moves (e.g. refinance vs move vs sell car) |
| Scenario planners | PocketSmith, ProjectionLab, Boldin, Monarch Plus Forecasting | "What if I do X?" when the user builds the scenario | The user must think up the scenario. Nothing proposes and **ranks** candidate moves |

| AI coaches | Cleo, Origin AI Advisor, Monarch AI Assistant, ChatGPT Finances, Credit Karma AI | Conversational answers and suggestions | Numbers are generated by or through an LLM, are not reproducible, mostly need a bank link, and are not a deterministic ranked list. ## Gaps we can honestly claim

1. **A ranked list of *your* next moves, scored by 10-year dollar effect from deterministic, tested code.** Planners (PocketSmith, ProjectionLab, Boldin, Monarch Plus) make the user *build* each scenario. AI coaches (Origin, Cleo, Monarch AI, ChatGPT) give LLM-generated suggestions. We found no product that auto-generates candidate moves across categories (loan refi, car, rent, childcare, savings) and ranks them by computed $.
2. **Decision tools in one profile.** Loan Lab (car, personal, student, BNPL, mortgage) plus "Can I Afford It?" plus a 10-year forecast share one household profile. NerdWallet and Bankrate calculators are single, stateless decisions.
3. **Useful with no bank login and no sign-up, end to end, for a 25–45 household budget.** ProjectionLab and PocketSmith also allow no linking, but they are retirement- or power-user-oriented and need heavy manual setup. Our onboarding defaults from public area data (HUD, Census, BLS) with "estimate" tags. Phrase this as "no bank login needed", not "the only app without bank login".
4. **A free daily cost-of-living guessing game as an acquisition channel tied to the product.** We found no game where you guess a stranger's monthly cost of living from an (anonymized) household profile and then estimate your own. Existing "-dle" games guess single prices (homes, rent, Costco items, cars, salaries). No money app in the table owns a viral game.
5. **Monthly forecast-vs-actual check-in.** This closes the loop on forecast accuracy. We saw no evidence that competitors report back how accurate their own forecast was, but this is **UNVERIFIED** as an absence.
6. **Price:** $39.99/yr undercuts every paid forecasting or planning competitor found.

## Claims to avoid

- ❌ "Budget apps only look backward." Monarch Plus (Apr 2026) sells forecasting, PocketSmith forecasts 60 years, and Copilot, Simplifi and Monarch Core project short-term cash flow.
- ❌ "We're the only app with no bank login." ProjectionLab cannot link at all. YNAB, PocketSmith, Monarch and Boldin allow manual accounts. Credit Karma needs no bank link.
- ❌ "No one forecasts decisions / what-ifs." Monarch Plus markets "what happens to my retirement age if I buy a $500k house" scenarios, and Origin markets "forecast scenarios" and "tells you what to do". Say instead: "they let you *model* a decision you already thought of; we *rank* the moves you haven't."
- ❌ "No finance game exists." There are many: MoneyRank (a daily ranking of money choices), Housle, Costcodle, Financhle, SalaryDuel, Spent, and Finelo's own gamified lessons and simulator.
- ❌ "No one uses real people's finances as content." Money Diaries, Salary Transparent Street and twocents do. The accurate claim is narrower: "no game built on anonymized household budgets that players guess".
- ❌ "AI financial advisor" framing, or recommending specific lenders or products. Origin is a registered advisor. We are an "educational simulation, not financial advice" (CLAUDE.md rule 7).
- ❌ Investing education, stock simulators, lesson streaks or a subscription canceller as features. That is Finelo, Prosperi and SubPilot territory, and the judges will see it as copying Zimran.
- ❌ Quoting the Monarch Plus price ($199 vs $299), the YNAB revenue, Rocket Money's subscriber count, or the Cleo GBP vs USD ARR as hard facts. They are unverified or conflicting.
- ❌ "Monarch makes $100M" as a Sacra-only figure. It is now CEO-stated ($100M **ARR**, ~Oct 2026), so cite it as ARR, not revenue.
