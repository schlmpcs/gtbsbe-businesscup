Method note: web search only. The fetch tool could not reach several primary hosts (businesswire.com, wordstream.com, a.storyblok.com, seodata.dev), so some figures are taken from search-result excerpts of the primary page or from secondary coverage. Each line says which. Most ad-cost "benchmarks" on the web are vendor blogs with undisclosed methods; they are labeled **(vendor, directional)**. Anything I could not confirm is marked **UNVERIFIED**.

## 6. CAC model

### Revenue per payer (sets the ceiling on CAC)
- Assumption: 70% pick annual ($39.99), 30% monthly ($6.99). Stripe fee ~2.9% + $0.30.
- Annual payer: ~$38.5 net year 1. Monthly payer: assume ~5 paid months in year 1 (assumption, not sourced) → ~$33 net.
- **Blended year-1 net revenue per payer ≈ $37.** A CAC above ~$37 does not pay back within 12 months.

### Conversion assumptions (stated)
- **App/web freemium visitor → paid: 2%** for engaged users, anchored on RevenueCat's freemium download→paid median of 2.1–2.2% (D35). Web visitors are colder than app installs, so for an **ad click** we assume **1% click → paid** (≈50% of clicks become engaged players × 2%).
- Search clicks have higher intent: **2% click → paid** (WordStream finance CVR 2.55% is for any conversion, e.g., a lead, so paid conversion should be lower).
- Creator-referred visitors are warmer: **2% visit → paid**.

### Blended CAC (illustrative)
Mix: 60% game/organic at $5, 15% SEO at $20, 10% referral at $7, 15% paid (Meta/TikTok/Reddit/creators) at $150
→ 0.60×5 + 0.15×20 + 0.10×7 + 0.15×150 = 3.0 + 3.0 + 0.7 + 22.5 = **≈ $29 per paying subscriber**, under the ~$37 year-1 revenue per payer (LTV/CAC ≈ 1.3 in year 1, better with annual renewals; RevenueCat 2026 says freemium annual plans keep ~28% after a year).

**Key pitch point:** paid social alone ($120–230 per payer) does not pay back at $39.99/yr. The model works only if the free game supplies most users near-zero cost, which is exactly what Wordle (share grid) and GeoGuessr (streamers) show. Paid spend is for testing creatives and retargeting game players, not for cold acquisition.
